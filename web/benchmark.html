01 / FOUR BASELINES · FROZEN TRAIN MODEL

Held-out model comparison

Hit Rate@5 measures the share of eligible users with at least one held-out positively rated item in the top five. NDCG@5 measures ranking position. Both are observational offline metrics.

Interpretation: The 60/40 hybrid is an untuned blend. Methods share the popularity fallback for users without positive training history; overall results do not isolate personalization.

03 / MODEL OBSERVABILITY

Prediction and performance drift

Compare later validation and test cohorts using exactly the same frozen model trained on the earlier period. Cohort composition can differ; the changes do not establish within-user deterioration.

Top-one score PSI and KS D describe cohort-level score shift, not concept drift or p-values. HR@5 differences use 300 bootstrap resamples of users in each cohort. A confidence interval containing zero is inconclusive.

04 / EVIDENCE & REPRODUCIBILITY

Protocol and provenance

Integrity of the evaluation

Global timestamp boundaries, no repeated user–item pair across partitions, train-only ranking features, and a catalog restricted to previously observed training items.

A user's positive rating is not proof they clicked an impression or purchased an item.

Data provenance

The official GroupLens archive was checksum-verified on an ephemeral GitHub Actions runner. Neither source files nor individual events are redistributed.
